Yoga

Time for myself.

Six main practices, each complete by themselves. Based on their first impression, arranged in three steps — cleanse and activate the system, strengthen the structure, and bring balance. No prior experience needed to learn any of them.

Step One

Cleanse & Activate

Cleanse the system and lubricate the body first in the morning to keep it well.

Cleansing · Elements

Bhuta Shuddhi

A daily practice of purifying the five elements within the body — working at the root of imbalances that lead to disease.

Bhuta Shuddhi is about removing everything that you have built up so that the Creator's creation will rise and shine within you.
— Sadhguru
1.5 hrs to learn10 min practice
Foundation · Beginners

Upa-Yoga

Ten simple yet powerful practices that activate the joints, muscles and energy system, releasing accumulated tensions and bringing ease to the whole system.

4-5 hrs to learn~30 min practice
Step Two

Strong, Flexible and Energetic

These practices slowly bring you there.

Flexibility · Fitness

Angamardana

A series of 31 processes to invigorate the body and cultivate peak physical and mental fitness — entirely without equipment.

Angamardana is a beautiful way of preparing the human mechanism for a higher possibility. Above all, it is one of the simplest and most powerful ways to gain fitness because it does not need any equipment or outside support.
— Sadhguru
11-14 hrs to learn~40 min practice
Strength · Ligaments

Surya Shakti

An 18-step sun-salutation sequence designed to energise the system and strengthen the ligaments that bind the skeletal and muscular structure.

3-4 hrs to learn1-3 min/cycle
Step Three

Bring Balance

Before stillness and silence, balance has to come.

Energy · Wellbeing

Surya Kriya

A potent 21-step yogic practice of tremendous antiquity, designed as a holistic process for health and inner wellbeing.

One should approach Surya Kriya with devotion and reverence – it is a powerful process. You can do it just to cure your backache, or you can use it to transform yourself absolutely. It has that potential.
— Sadhguru
6-8 hrs to learn~20 min/cycle
Postures · Awareness

Yogasanas

A classical set of powerful postures through which one can elevate consciousness and work with the body's energy structure.

10-12 hrs to learn1-1.5 hrs practice

Common Questions

What do I need to start?

Not needed.

Comfortable clothing that allows free movement. No props, or other equipments are required.

It is best to start with a practice that suits your health, physical situation and available time. I can help you find a program that suits your needs.

Classes are held in Kollbrunn, Zurich, and can also be hosted at a preferred location on request — including for small groups or corporate settings.

All classes are taught in English. A German version of this site is also available.

These are gentle, foundational practices suitable for most people, but everyone's body is different. If you have an injury, a health condition, or any concern, please reach out before your first session so we can talk it through — and always check with your doctor if you're uncertain.

From the beginning, the classes designed to teach you how to practice them properly on your own. So you can continue them at home. But please reach out if you have a question about the practice or need a review or correction.

These are one time cost for a tool for your health and wellbeing for a lifetime. Join a free introduction webinar to know more.

The tradition

The origin

Sadhguru

Yogi, Mystic & Founder of Isha Foundation

The practices offered here are structured by Sadhguru.

Sadhguru is a yogi, mystic and visionary, and a prominent spiritual leader. An author, poet, and internationally-renowned speaker, Sadhguru's wit and piercing logic provoke and widen our perception of life.

Sadhguru is also the founder of Isha Foundation, a non-profit organization which has been dedicated to the wellbeing of the individual and the world for the past three decades. Isha Foundation does not promote any particular ideology, religion, or race, but transmits inner sciences of universal appeal.

Isha Foundation

Sadhguru Gurukulam

Hatha Yoga Teacher Training Program

Sadhguru Gurukulam delivers classical Hatha Yoga in its full depth and dimension. It is Sadhguru's vision to offer this ancient science in all its purity and make it available to every individual. As a step towards realizing this vision, he has devised the Hatha Yoga Teacher Training Program.

In this program, Hatha Yoga is taught as a living experience in the most beautiful ashram setting of the Isha Yoga Center, India, under the grace of a living master. Upon completion of the program, trainees have the privilege and fulfillment of bringing this knowledge to many more people.
