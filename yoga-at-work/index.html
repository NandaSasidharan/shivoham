For the workplace

Yoga at Work

Learn gentle practices for your wellbeing at the workplace. Office work need not degrade your physical health. With Yoga, you can also rise to a new level of competence and clarity.

Workshops
Picture: Yoga at Work

Success and wellbeing are two important aspects of work life in corporates and offices. Often in pursuit of success, we fail to care for our own well-being. Classical Hatha Yoga offers practices that enhance clarity and competence while taking care of physical and mental wellbeing.

Small breaks, taken regularly, add up. A few minutes of mindful movement can leave you clearer, more focused and more at ease, for yourself and for the people you work with.

Available for offices in the Zurich region, including Kollbrunn and Winterthur.

If you think your work is important, it is very important that you work upon yourself.

— Sadhguru

Let us encourage a regular 2-minute spine stretch break in the offices, so that desk jobs do not lead to backaches.
