Bhuta Shuddhi

The basis for all creation, including the physical body, is the group of five elements: earth, water, wind, fire, and space. The wellbeing of the body and mind can be established by purifying these five elements within the human system. This process also shapes the body into a stepping stone towards one’s ultimate wellbeing.

1.5 hrs to learn10 min practiceCHF 200

Mantra Yoga

There is a beautiful story in the yogic lore that describes how when Adiyogi utters three AUMs, a whole new creation comes into being. The story is a dialectical expression of the possibility of the sound AUM. Known as the universal or basic sound in the existence, AUM is a combination of the three primordial syllables – “Aaa”, “Ooo”, and “Maa”. AUM chanting is a powerful practice that aligns the three fundamental aspects of the system – body, mind and energy – leading to a more profound experience of life. It helps stabilize the body and mind, thus eliminating psychological disturbances and alleviating chronic ailments. AUM chanting creates a powerful basis for further inner exploration.

Benefits
  • Helps stabilize the body and mind
  • Helps come out of psychological disturbances
  • Relief from excessive fear, nightmares, unstable mind
  • Remedies attention disorders
  • Helps bring resolve in life

Jala Neti

A yogic cleansing method that removes excess mucus and pollutants from nasal passages and sinuses.

Benefits
  • Helps with the diseases of eyes, nose, throat
  • Helps relieve insomnia, tiredness, improves quality of sleep
  • Prevents and helps with respiratory tract diseases like asthma, pneumonia, bronchitis
  • Relieves cold, allergies, sinusitis
  • Helps with tonsillitis, allergic rhinitis, hay fever
  • Relieve migraine and headaches, anxiety, stress release and brings tranquility
  • Helps with epilepsy
Picture: Shanmukhi Mudra

Shanmukhi Mudra

Simple but subtle practice that brightens and rejuvenates the face and eyes. Brings balance leading toward increased awareness and meditativeness.

Benefits
  • Improves vision
  • Brightens the eyes and face. It improves the aura in the face. People concerned about their beauty can do it, actors can do it.
  • Helps turn a person inward, prepares one for pratyahara.
  • Rejuvenates different parts of the face, including helping with ailments related to nose, ears, eyes.
  • Helps relieve vertigo and tinnitus
  • Enhances awareness
  • Brings about mental balance
